# Long-Term History — template

> Lives in Coach MCP as the variable `long_term_history`. This is the multi-year narrative: how the athlete has developed, and the notable things — illness, injury, breakthroughs, major camps, race results — that explain the shape of their long-term progression. Updated rarely: season transitions, major health events, or when old entries roll off `short_term_history` and are worth preserving. Read the current version fresh every session; don't assume the copy in context is current.

**Last updated:** _(YYYY-MM-DD)_

---

## Purpose

This file answers questions like "why does this athlete respond the way they do to X" and "has this happened before." It is not a training log — that level of detail lives in `short_term_history` while it's recent, and is compressed to a sentence or two here once it ages out. Keep entries short and information-dense; this file should stay readable end-to-end in a couple of minutes even after years of use.
